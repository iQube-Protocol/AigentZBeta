# CFS-052 — The Crucible

**Chrysalis Foundation · Constitutional Research Charter**  
**Status:** RATIFIED  
**Ratified by:** Operator  
**Ratification date:** 2026-08-20  
**Depends on / composes:** CFS-051; `types/research.ts` / `EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY`; `canonical-invariants.seed.json`; `appendix-a_canonical-invariants.md`; IRL-001; IRL-002; IRL-010; IRL-010A.  
**Supersession rule:** This charter canonizes the name and function of the pre-canon substrate. It does not alter the formal experiment registry, invariant canonization ceremony, constitutional ratification process, or evidence requirements.

---

## 1. Canonical name

The collective pre-canon research and development substrate is henceforth named:

# **The Crucible**

Canonical operational phrase:

> **Commit this to the Crucible.**

Meaning: preserve an idea as a first-class candidate object with provenance, status, dependencies and an appropriate path to testing, implementation, validation, falsification or canonization — without treating it as canonical merely because it has been captured.

## 2. Canonical distinction

> **The Crucible is where propositions are tested. The Crystal is what survives.**

The Crucible and the Crystal are complementary and must not be conflated.

- **The Crucible** is the pre-canon field of inquiry, challenge, experimentation, perturbation, prototyping and development.
- **The Crystal** is durable invariant memory: the substrate in which validated/canonized invariants are retained and made available for governed reuse.
- **Canon** remains the formally ratified body produced only through the applicable existing constitutional/research ceremony.

A Crucible entry is therefore not evidence of truth, validity, implementation or ratification. It is evidence that a proposition has been deliberately preserved for disciplined treatment.

## 3. Scope

The Crucible may contain, at minimum:

1. candidate invariants;
2. candidate constitutional principles;
3. hypotheses and research questions;
4. proposed equations, relations and models;
5. candidate experiments and experimental protocols;
6. perturbations, ablations and falsification tests;
7. negative findings and failed hypotheses worth retaining;
8. unresolved questions and research backlog items;
9. implementation hypotheses and technical prototypes;
10. development workstreams and code roadmaps;
11. dependencies between research, constitutional and engineering objects;
12. evidence awaiting reconciliation, review or promotion.

The Crucible is deliberately broader than an Experiment Registry and broader than an Invariant Discovery Ledger. Those are object classes or views within the larger Crucible concept.

## 4. Relationship to CFS-051

CFS-051 established the existing **Experiment / Constitutional / Invariant Pipeline** as the pre-formal place for candidate experiments, candidate principles, candidate invariants and research backlog items while explicitly preserving the formal `EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY` and invariant canon as sole authorities for their respective ratified objects.

**The Crucible is the canonical collective name for that pre-canon function and its future research/development extensions.**

CFS-051's extend-don't-duplicate boundary remains binding:

- The Crucible does **not** become a second formal experiment registry.
- The Crucible does **not** become a second invariant canon.
- The Crucible does **not** canonize an invariant by capture.
- The Crucible does **not** ratify a constitutional principle by capture.
- The Crucible does **not** declare code implemented merely because a roadmap or prototype is recorded.

Existing formal promotion and evidence ceremonies remain unchanged.

## 5. Lifecycle

Canonical conceptual lifecycle:

`Discovery → Crucible → Experiment / Perturbation / Prototype → Evidence → Validation or Falsification → Crystal / Canon / Code`

Not every object follows every branch.

Examples:

- A candidate invariant may be falsified and retained as negative evidence.
- A research question may become one or more formal experiments.
- A proposed equation may be revised into a different functional form.
- A technical hypothesis may become a prototype and then production code.
- A development roadmap may be completed without producing a canonical invariant.
- A validated invariant may be promoted through the existing canonization ceremony into the Crystal/canon.

The Crucible is therefore a **branching proving substrate**, not a one-way publication queue.

## 6. Evidence discipline

The Crucible operates under the existing IRL discipline of falsifiability and evidentiary honesty.

A Crucible object SHOULD identify, where applicable:

- proposition / object type;
- provenance and date;
- current status;
- dependencies;
- governing invariants or constitutional constraints;
- test or implementation required;
- perturbations / counter-hypotheses;
- falsification conditions;
- evidence generated;
- evidence classification;
- disposition: retained, revised, falsified, promoted, implemented, archived;
- canonical or code reference after promotion.

Unsupported propositions are not defended by virtue of being in the Crucible. Contradictory evidence is retained. Revision supersedes rather than silently rewriting prior states.

## 7. Research and development seam

The Crucible explicitly spans both **research** and **development**.

Research asks whether a proposition survives contact with evidence. Development asks whether a proposition survives contact with implementation and consequence. Both can reveal invariants, risks, failure modes and negative evidence.

Accordingly, code roadmaps and implementation hypotheses may be committed to the Crucible when they are part of an unresolved constitutional, invariant or experimental question. Their implementation state must remain distinct from their research status.

This allows a common vocabulary across IRL, IDE, DevOn, DCIR and Crystal without collapsing their distinct roles.

## 8. Operational language

The following language is canonical:

- **"Commit it to the Crucible"** — capture as a candidate object without conferring canonical status.
- **"In the Crucible"** — currently under inquiry, scoping, testing, perturbation, implementation or evaluation.
- **"Run it through the Crucible"** — subject the proposition to the appropriate experimental/development/evidentiary pathway.
- **"Survived the Crucible"** — obtained the evidence required for the next applicable promotion ceremony; this phrase alone does not bypass that ceremony.
- **"Failed in the Crucible"** — falsified or materially contradicted; retain as negative evidence where useful.
- **"Crystallized"** — promoted into durable invariant memory only after the existing required validation/canonization process.

## 9. Mythos and function

The name is functional, not decorative.

A crucible is a vessel in which material is subjected to conditions capable of transforming it and revealing whether it retains integrity. The research analogue is deliberate: propositions are not protected from pressure; they are exposed to it.

Thus:

> **IRL operates the Crucible. Experiments and implementation apply the pressure. Evidence determines what survives. The Crystal retains what has earned durability.**

## 10. Ratification

On 2026-08-20 the operator explicitly selected **The Crucible** and authorized its canonization.

This ratification establishes the name, scope, distinction and operational vocabulary in this charter. It does not retroactively ratify any candidate invariant, equation, experiment, roadmap or claim currently contained within the Crucible.
