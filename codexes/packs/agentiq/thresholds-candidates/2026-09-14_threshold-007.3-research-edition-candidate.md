# Abstract

The dominant approach to increasing artificial-intelligence capability emphasizes scale: more compute, data, model capacity, inference-time search, tool use, memory, agentic coordination, parallelism and reasoning.

This paper proposes **Invariant Intelligence** as a complementary research thesis:

> **What if intelligence advances not only by increasing the resources available for reasoning, but by changing where reasoning begins?**

The thesis asks whether accumulated, falsifiable, scoped and reopenable structure can become reusable computational substrate such that prior reasoning no longer has to be repeatedly reconstructed before novel reasoning can begin.

The weak form of the thesis is **Structural Compression**: invariant structure reduces rediscovery cost.

The stronger form is **Frontier Extension**: under resource budgets matched for information, compute and time, invariant-assisted reasoning can reach problems unavailable to a baseline system before the same resource ceiling is exhausted.

The deeper form is **Constitutional Cybernetic Compounding**: reusable structural invariance, human-machine cybernetic learning and constitutional conditions interact non-additively, allowing intelligence to become more consequentially capable without permitting action reach to outrun warranted consequence reach.

Threshold 007.3 therefore normalizes the theory into four principal hypotheses:

\[
H1 — Structural\ Invariance
\]

\[
H2 — Cybernetic\ Invariance
\]

\[
H3 — Constitutional\ Invariance
\]

\[
H4 — Constitutional\ Cybernetic\ Compounding
\]

The paper also makes five major refinements to the predecessor research architecture.

First, invariants are distinguished across **four independent axes**: Class, Polarity, Epistemic State and Consequential Bearing. Representation is treated separately.

Second, the paper elevates **Invariant Risk** from an edge case to a central property of computational reliance. The more reusable an invariant becomes, the greater both its potential value and its potential blast radius. Invariant Intelligence therefore requires discovery, compression **and reopening**.

Third, **Constitutional Cybernetics** becomes the explicit bridge between structural invariance and personhood. Once computation becomes consequential, the cybernetic loop has a subject. The person is treated as constitutional principal, intended beneficiary, consequence bearer and privileged consequence sensor.

Fourth, the paper distinguishes **exploratory cognition** from **consequential action**. The constitutional event is not cognition itself but the conversion of capability into action. Hence: **Free Cognition. Bounded Agency.** Deception remains a serious adversarial condition rather than something constitutionality is assumed to abolish.

Fifth, the paper explicitly records its own adversarial status: the theory merits falsification, not belief.

The resulting research programme is still empirically nascent. Existing experiments establish bounded findings only. No current result establishes matched-resource frontier extension, tacit-consequence discovery, constitutionally improved signal integrity, Consequence Horizon measurement, non-additive H4 interaction, or Trusted Superintelligence.

The purpose of the Research Edition is therefore not to certify Invariant Intelligence.

It is to make the thesis easier to kill.

# 1. Research Problem: Reasoning at the Frontier

Contemporary AI capability development is dominated by an additive paradigm:

\[
C = f(compute,data,architecture,inference,tools,agents,time)
\]

Threshold 007 does not contest that paradigm.

Scaling works.

Nothing in the present research record establishes that Invariant Intelligence will outperform continued scaling.

The narrower proposition is that conventional accounts may omit another potentially scalable resource:

\[
C = f(scale,reusable\ reasoning\ structure)
\]

Reasoning is not computationally free.

Before a system reaches the genuinely uncertain portion of a problem, it may have to recover context, identify relevant relationships, reject impossible alternatives, re-establish constraints, determine which prior conclusions remain applicable and rediscover structure traversed in earlier reasoning.

Some of what appears to be reasoning is therefore the cost of getting to the place where reasoning is still necessary.

Threshold 007 calls that place the **reasoning frontier**: the boundary at which reusable established structure ceases to be sufficient and novel reasoning becomes necessary.

The research objective is not simply to reason less. It is to investigate whether more finite reasoning expenditure can be concentrated where reasoning remains valuable.

A baseline process may be represented as:

\[
Problem \rightarrow Search \rightarrow Reason \rightarrow Evaluate \rightarrow Answer
\]

An invariant-assisted process becomes:

\[
Problem \rightarrow Invariant\ Field \rightarrow Constrained\ Search \rightarrow Novel\ Reasoning \rightarrow Answer
\]

The invariant field is not intended to provide the answer. It supplies **pre-reasoning**.

This yields the central research question:

> **What if the fastest path to Superintelligence is not simply more intelligence, but more invariance?**

The proposition remains interrogative. The thesis has become more precise. It has not become proven.

# 2. Epistemic and Evidentiary Framework

Threshold Research Editions distinguish five evidence classes.

## 2.1 Doctrine

A constitutional proposition, formal architecture, ratified conceptual distinction or project doctrine. Doctrine can define what a system is intended to mean or require. Doctrine does not establish that a mechanism works empirically.

## 2.2 Implementation

Machinery that instantiates a proposition or makes it testable. Implementation establishes that a mechanism or instrument exists at the stated maturity. It does not establish that the scientific thesis represented by that mechanism is true.

## 2.3 Research Candidate / Crucible

A falsifiable proposition, candidate invariant, mechanism hypothesis or experiment family awaiting adjudication. This is the default status for substantive new claims introduced in 007.3 unless an existing evidence record justifies stronger classification.

## 2.4 Research Evidence / IRL

Evidence produced under registered scientific or constitutional research controls, subject strictly to the scope and limits of the relevant protocol. A result supports only the claim the experiment was designed and sufficiently powered to test.

## 2.5 Operational Evidence / Venture Lab

Consequential deployment evidence, including usage, outcomes, failure, repair and economics. Operational evidence answers whether something survives consequence. It is not automatically confirmatory scientific evidence.

The governing distinction remains:

> **Scientific validation asks whether a mechanism survives controlled falsification. Operational validation asks whether it survives consequence. Neither substitutes for the other.**

The associated research loop is:

\[
Crucible \rightarrow IRL \rightarrow Constitutional\ Evidence \rightarrow Runtime \rightarrow Venture\ Lab \rightarrow Realized\ Consequence \rightarrow Evidence \rightarrow Crucible/IRL
\]

Operational telemetry becomes scientific evidence only when captured through a registered protocol with controlled provenance.

# 3. Thesis Strength: Weak, Strong and Deep

The thesis should not be treated as one indivisible proposition.

## 3.1 Weak Thesis — Structural Compression

Scoped, evidenced and reopenable invariant structure reduces the cost of reasoning already within reach. This is approximately H1a. It is the least novel and most plausible layer.

If the programme establishes only this effect, Invariant Intelligence may still be a useful reasoning architecture without establishing a distinct route to greater intelligence.

## 3.2 Strong Thesis — Frontier Extension

Under matched information, compute and time, accumulated invariant structure changes what can be solved within a fixed resource budget.

This is approximately H1b.

The effect is not merely the same problem, more cheaply. It is a problem beyond the baseline ceiling becoming reachable because validated structure no longer consumes the same budget to reconstruct.

## 3.3 Deep Thesis — Constitutional Cybernetic Compounding

Structural invariance, cybernetic learning and constitutional conditions interact non-additively such that the integrated system generates consequential intelligence beyond the sum of its components.

This is H4.

It is the most ambitious claim and the least established.

A reader may reject H4 while retaining H1a, H1b or parts of H2/H3. That separability is intentional.

# MOVEMENT I — STRUCTURAL INVARIANCE

# 4. Formal Construct: The Invariant

Invariant Intelligence begins with a deceptively simple question:

> **What should remain true within defined conditions?**

For research purposes an invariant must not be treated as immutable truth.

A candidate invariant should minimally carry:

\[
I = \{Structure,Scope,Evidence,Falsifiability,Lineage,Reopening\}
\]

This is an analytical definition, not a claim about the final production schema.

A more complete research object for 007.3 can be represented as:

\[
I = \{Class,Polarity,EpistemicState,ConsequentialBearing,Representation,Scope,Evidence,Version,Dependencies,ReopeningConditions\}
\]

The first four elements describe independent axes of the invariant. Representation is architecturally separate. The remaining fields establish the invariant's evidentiary and operational contract.

The governing principle is:

> **An invariant earns the right to compress reasoning only for as long as it survives evidence capable of reopening it.**

---

# 5. Four Axes of Invariance

007.3 distinguishes four independent dimensions that must not be collapsed.

## 5.1 Axis One — Class

### Structural

A structural invariant concerns causal, relational, logical, computational or otherwise persistent structure.

It asks:

> What appears to remain true within these conditions?

### Constitutional

A constitutional invariant concerns admissibility of consequential computation.

It asks:

> What must remain true for consequential action to remain legitimate?

This distinction is conceptually load-bearing.

**Evidence Agent requirement:** determine whether structural-versus-constitutional kind is explicitly machine-readable in current implementation, partly represented, or doctrine-only. Do not infer enforcement from conceptual language.

## 5.2 Axis Two — Polarity

### Positive invariant

\[
Something\ Must\ Hold
\]

### Negative invariant

\[
Something\ Must\ Not\ Hold
\]

Negative does not mean undesirable. Positive does not mean true. Polarity describes the direction of a constraint, not its epistemic quality or value.

## 5.3 Axis Three — Epistemic State

Candidate states include Supported, Uncertain, Challenged and Falsified.

Production lifecycle terms may differ. The research distinction is that confidence and falsification state are independent of polarity.

A positive invariant may be falsified. A negative invariant may be well supported. No invariant is rendered metaphysically permanent by entering a supported state.

## 5.4 Axis Four — Consequential Bearing

Reliance on an invariant may be value-creating, risk-introducing, or both.

A valid invariant may still introduce substantial risk because the consequences of misapplication are severe. A false invariant may appear useful before its downstream repair burden becomes visible.

This axis therefore concerns what computational reliance does, not whether the proposition is positive, negative or currently supported.

## 5.5 Representation Is Separate

An invariant may be represented as graph, relation, rule, executable primitive, natural-language proposition, causal model, constraint, proof object or another machine-readable structure.

Representation is an implementation question. It should not be silently treated as a fifth epistemic axis.

# 6. The Invariant Field

For a reasoning problem \(x\), define:

\[
\mathcal I_x = \{I_1,I_2,\dots,I_n\}
\]

where each invariant is admitted only within its scope and current epistemic state.

The research proposition is that previously expended reasoning may be preserved not merely as memory but as reusable structure.

> **Memory preserves information. Invariance preserves structure.**

This distinction is itself experimental.

A strong H1 protocol therefore requires an **information-matched control**. Without such a control, an observed gain may simply result from giving the invariant-assisted system more useful information.

The minimum discriminating comparison is therefore not invariant system versus no memory, but invariant structure versus equivalent underlying information, with compute and time also controlled.

# 7. H1 — Structural Invariance

\[
\boxed{H1 — Structural\ Invariance}
\]

Can reusable invariant structure reduce rediscovery cost and, more strongly, extend the reachable reasoning frontier under matched resources?

## 7.1 H1a — Structural Compression

**Hypothesis II0073-H1a**

Invariant-assisted reasoning can reduce reasoning expenditure on appropriate problem classes while preserving or improving quality, novelty, contradiction detection and reopening.

A first-order condition is:

\[
Cost_I < Cost_B
\]

at equivalent or superior quality.

This is useful. It is not yet frontier extension.

### Primary falsifiers

- no reproducible saving;
- lower quality;
- novelty suppression;
- false-invariant entrenchment;
- invariant-management overhead exceeds downstream savings;
- advantage disappears against an information-matched strong retrieval/memory control.

## 7.2 H1b — Frontier Extension

**Hypothesis II0073-H1b**

Under fixed and matched information, compute and time budgets, invariant-assisted reasoning can solve problems unavailable to the baseline system before the same resource ceiling is exhausted.

Conceptually:

\[
Frontier_I > Frontier_B
\]

under:

\[
Information_I = Information_B
\]

\[
Compute_I \approx Compute_B
\]

\[
Time_I \approx Time_B
\]

This is the strong thesis.

The boundary condition matters. If both systems can already solve the target problem within budget, the result may demonstrate efficiency but not frontier extension.

The strongest experiment therefore identifies a resource ceiling at which the baseline fails while the invariant condition succeeds, then tests whether that difference survives replication, equivalent-information controls and adversarial task construction.

### Primary falsifier

The frontier advantage disappears once underlying information, compute and time are properly matched.

# 8. Recursive Floors

The deeper possibility is recursive:

\[
Reason_n \rightarrow Discovery_n \rightarrow Validation_n \rightarrow Invariant_n \rightarrow Substrate_{n+1} \rightarrow Reason_{n+1}
\]

or:

\[
Frontier_n \rightarrow Discovery_n \rightarrow Invariant_n \rightarrow Frontier_{n+1}
\]

The system becomes more capable not only because the reasoner improves, but because validated discovery changes the floor from which later reasoning begins.

This is a candidate mechanism.

It becomes scientifically interesting only if the effect survives amortized accounting for invariant discovery cost, validation cost, maintenance cost, reopening cost, false-invariant cost, and representation/retrieval overhead.

A downstream saving cannot be treated as net intelligence gain if the substrate costs more to construct and maintain than the reasoning it replaces.

# MOVEMENT II — THE CYBERNETIC INVARIANT

# 9. Invariant Risk

The strongest objection surfaced by adversarial review is not external to the thesis. It follows directly from the compression mechanism.

Compression works because something is **not re-derived in full** every time it is used.

That creates leverage. Leverage creates both value and exposure.

007.3 therefore introduces the governing proposition:

> **Invariant Risk begins when validated structure is granted computational reliance.**

Risk does not begin only when the invariant becomes false.

Potential sources include false invariance, context drift, inappropriate scope extension, propagation depth, composition, dependency concentration, irreversibility, asymmetric consequence, overconfidence, delayed contradiction and incentive resistance to reopening.

The more reusable an invariant becomes, the greater both its potential value and potential blast radius.

Conceptually:

\[
Invariant\ Privilege \rightarrow \{Potential\ Value,Potential\ Risk\}
\]

This creates a second-order research requirement: the system must not only discover structure. It must detect when reliance on that structure has become unsafe.

---

# 10. Reopening

Invariant Intelligence cannot solve Invariant Risk by reasoning everything from first principles forever. That would destroy the compression mechanism.

Nor can it solve the problem by treating admission as permanent settlement. That would convert invariance into dogma.

A research lifecycle may therefore resemble:

\[
Candidate \rightarrow Tested \rightarrow Active \rightarrow Watch \rightarrow Challenged \rightarrow Reopened \rightarrow Revised/Retired
\]

The exact production states remain an implementation question. The principle is not.

> **Falsifiability does not end when an invariant is admitted. Admission begins its continuing burden of falsifiability.**

This yields a stronger formulation:

\[
\boxed{Invariant\ Intelligence = Discovery + Compression + Reopening}
\]

And a stronger test:

> **The intelligence of an invariant lies not only in what it allows a system to stop reasoning about, but in whether the system can discover when it must begin reasoning about it again.**

# 11. H2 — Cybernetic Invariance

Static invariants are stored structure. Invariant Intelligence requires a feedback loop.

\[
Invariant \rightarrow Reasoning \rightarrow Action \rightarrow Consequence \rightarrow Evidence \rightarrow Invariant'
\]

This gives the second principal hypothesis:

\[
\boxed{H2 — Cybernetic\ Invariance}
\]

Can human reasoning, machine reasoning and consequence generate, test, reuse and reopen invariant structure?

H2 is broader than the predecessor's “Cybernetic Amplification” formulation. Amplification is a possible outcome. Cybernetic Invariance describes the mechanism to be tested.

## 11.1 H2a — Reusable Human Reasoning

Can a useful human insight become computationally reusable without requiring synchronous human intervention each time it matters?

\[
Human\ Insight \rightarrow Candidate\ Invariant \rightarrow Machine\ Testing \rightarrow Reusable\ Structure
\]

The critical negative test remains:

> Does computationalizing human reasoning remove the tacit, contextual or interpretive properties that made the contribution useful?

If yes across the intended domain, this branch of H2 weakens.

## 11.2 H2b — Reusable Machine Reasoning

Can machine-discovered structure become an inspectable object of human interpretation, challenge and refinement rather than remaining an opaque downstream artifact?

Candidate loop:

\[
Machine\ Discovery \rightarrow Candidate\ Structure \rightarrow Human\ Interpretation \rightarrow Challenge/Rescoping \rightarrow Reusable\ Invariant
\]

This is not assumed superior to machine-only reasoning. It requires direct controlled comparison.

## 11.3 H2c — Tacit Expertise

A person may repeatedly demonstrate a capability they cannot fully articulate.

\[
Experience \rightarrow Latent\ Capability \rightarrow Candidate\ Invariant
\]

Research question:

> Can the system discover a capability the person did not explicitly declare, infer the conditions in which it appears, and successfully predict its usefulness in a new context?

This is stronger than preference prediction. It is a transfer claim.

## 11.4 H2d — Tacit Consequence

A consequential system may produce effects nobody originally thought to measure.

\[
Intervention \rightarrow Experience \rightarrow Latent\ Consequential\ Pattern \rightarrow Candidate\ Structure
\]

Tacit expertise discovers hidden structure in **capability**. Tacit consequence discovers hidden structure in **effect**.

This yields the distinction:

\[
Measured\ Outcome \neq Total\ Consequence
\]

Research question:

> **Can longitudinal experience reveal consequences that neither the person, system nor institution originally knew to look for?**

And:

> **Can constitutional cybernetics shorten the interval between the emergence of an unmodeled consequence and its recognition as consequential structure?**

No existing experiment currently establishes this.

## 11.5 H2e — Reopening Under Drift

A valid invariant at \(t_0\) may become unsafe without a clean falsifying event.

The required experiment therefore includes gradual context drift, low-frequency contradiction or adversarial slow-poisoning.

Measure time to detection, downstream propagation before detection, repair burden, successful reopening, dependency traceability and retained value of compression.

This is a direct attempt to falsify whether the proposed reopening architecture is sufficient for the mechanism it enables.

# 12. Human-in-the-Invariant

A conventional sequential human-in-the-loop architecture may be represented as:

\[
Machine \rightarrow Stop \rightarrow Human \rightarrow Decision \rightarrow Resume
\]

Invariant-mediated reasoning instead proposes:

\[
Human\ Insight \rightarrow Candidate\ Invariant \rightarrow Computational\ Representation \rightarrow Machine\ Testing \rightarrow Machine\ Application
\]

and recursively:

\[
Human\ Reasoning \rightarrow Candidate\ Invariant \rightarrow Machine\ Testing \rightarrow Consequence \rightarrow Human\ Interpretation \rightarrow Refined\ Invariant
\]

The proposition is not merely “keep humans in the loop.” It is:

> **put human reasoning in the invariant.**

This does not mean every human judgment should be made permanent or authoritative. It means useful discovery by one form of intelligence may become testable substrate for another.

Human and machine intelligence remain differently shaped. The empirical question is not whether the hybrid sounds desirable. It is whether invariant-mediated Human × Machine conditions outperform relevant human-only, machine-only and conventional human-in-the-loop baselines on the variables claimed.

# MOVEMENT III — CONSTITUTIONAL CYBERNETICS

# 13. Consequence Introduces a Subject

A cybernetic system learns through feedback. But once computation acts consequentially, feedback is not abstract. Someone or something bears the consequence.

This introduces the question:

> **Consequence for whom?**

That is the point at which cybernetics becomes constitutional.

The Constitutional Internet lineage treats the person not merely as user, data source or optimization target, but as principal.

The person may be constitutional subject, intended beneficiary, consequence bearer, consequence sensor, consequence discoverer and cybernetic participant.

This is not a claim that subjective report is infallible. It is a claim that some dimensions of human consequence are not fully observable from external telemetry alone.

Where computational consequence is ultimately human consequence, the person may therefore be a **privileged consequence sensor**.

# 14. H3 — Constitutional Invariance

\[
\boxed{H3 — Constitutional\ Invariance}
\]

Can constitutional conditions improve legitimate consequential learning while governing the conversion of capability into authority?

H3 replaces the narrower predecessor formulation “Constitutional Expansion.” Expansion may occur. The core mechanism is constitutional invariance: what must remain true as cognition becomes consequential action.

H3 contains several separable research questions.

## 14.1 H3a — Constitutional Subject and Authority

Does personhood-bound authority allow computational systems to distinguish principal from persona, capability from authority, delegated scope from raw technical possibility, action from permitted action, and consequence from legitimate consequence?

The governing distinctions are:

\[
Personhood \neq Identity
\]

\[
Capability \neq Authority
\]

\[
Access \neq Authority
\]

A system may technically be able to act without possessing a legitimate mandate to do so.

## 14.2 Personhood Is Not Identity

Personhood establishes continuity of the constitutional principal. Identity is a contextual or social representation of that principal.

\[
Personhood = Continuous\ Principal
\]

\[
Identity = Contextual\ Representation
\]

The person may change roles, credentials, institutions, pseudonyms and personas while remaining the same constitutional subject.

This distinction matters for longitudinal cybernetics. A system should be able to preserve experience across time without requiring every experience to collapse into permanently exposed identity.

**Evidence Agent requirement:** map the exact doctrinal and implementation lineage for personhood, identity/persona separation, and any relevant DID/Passport machinery. Do not claim complete production enforcement where only doctrine or partial implementation exists.

## 14.3 Standing Is Not Reputation

\[
Standing \neq Reputation
\]

Reputation concerns how an identity is regarded. Standing concerns consequentially evidenced capacity or position within a constitutional system.

Candidate loop:

\[
Action_t \rightarrow Consequence_t \rightarrow Evidence_t \rightarrow Standing_{t+1} \rightarrow Authority_{t+1}
\]

The last arrow is a research and governance surface, not a universal empirical law.

Standing must not be described as unconditionally irrevocable. Person-level continuity and participant/agent rights require precise treatment from the canonical Standing doctrine.

**Evidence Agent requirement:** resolve exact doctrine and current code paths; distinguish person-level Standing from agent/participant-held or otherwise revocable rights; distinguish person Standing from unrelated invariant-confidence scores that may share the term.

## 14.4 Personal Agency Is Not Personalization

\[
Personal\ Agency \neq Personalization
\]

A personalization architecture often proceeds:

\[
Observation \rightarrow Inference \rightarrow Profile \rightarrow Predicted\ Preference \rightarrow Intervention
\]

A personal-agency architecture begins:

\[
Person \rightarrow Intent \rightarrow Delegation \rightarrow Assistance \rightarrow Experience
\]

This is a constitutional inversion.

The person is not principally the object of computation. The person becomes the source of legitimate computational direction.

The distinction does not eliminate inference. It changes its constitutional order.

> **Personal agency does not eliminate personalization. It subordinates personalization to personhood, intent and delegated authority.**

## 14.5 Intent Is a Compass

Intent is not a complete model of the person.

\[
Intent \neq Total\ Experience
\]

A person may discover capabilities, preferences, consequences or opportunities they could not state in advance.

Intent therefore provides direction without predetermining what experience can teach.

> **Intent gives experience direction. It does not predetermine what experience teaches.**

This distinction is required for declared agency and tacit discovery to coexist.

# MOVEMENT IV — THE CONSEQUENCE FRONTIER

# 19. Time as a Cross-Domain Coordinate

Consequence needs some means of comparison across domains.

Finance provides a powerful representation of value:

\[
Price
\]

Price allows heterogeneous goods, services and expectations to be expressed through a common economic representation.

But price is not universal. Some valued experiences have no meaningful market price. Some losses cannot adequately be priced.

Time may provide a more general coordinate.

Not because:

\[
Time = Value
\]

It does not.

Rather, value and consequence are repeatedly encountered through time: time gained, time lost, time spent searching, time spent waiting, time required to repair, opportunity delayed, and time returned for self-authored experience.

The candidate proposition is:

> **If price is one way markets compress value, time may be one way intelligence encounters value across domains.**

Time is therefore proposed as a **cross-domain coordinate**, not a universal scalar of value.

---

# 20. Time-to-Value and Risk-of-Repair

**Time-to-Value (TTV)** asks:

> How much time separates an intention from the realization of a valuable consequence?

**Risk-of-Repair (RoR)** asks:

> What future claims on time, resources, agency or consequence are created if the present action proves mistaken?

The governing discipline is:

> **Time saved must not be purchased by exporting repair.**

A useful analytical condition is:

\[
\Delta TTV < 0
\]

subject to:

\[
\Delta RoR \leq \epsilon
\]

for a preregistered acceptable repair bound \(\epsilon\).

This is not a ratified universal equation.

RoR does not currently possess one universally calibrated scalar form. Nor should existing Proof-of-Time-Saved formulations be silently collapsed into a single canonical formula where the historical record contains multiple formulations.

The present research objective is narrower:

> **compress Time-to-Value without increasing or exporting unacceptable Risk-of-Repair.**

Applied to invariants:

\[
TTV_{with\ invariant} < TTV_{without\ invariant}
\]

may indicate useful compression, but only if acceleration does not create disproportionate future repair.

# 22. Consequence Horizon

Cybernetics learns after action. Trusted agency must also reason before action.

Retrospective loop:

\[
Action \rightarrow Consequence \rightarrow Evidence \rightarrow Learning
\]

Prospective loop:

\[
Proposed\ Action \rightarrow Projected\ Consequence \rightarrow Authority\ Decision \rightarrow Action
\]

007.3 defines **Consequence Horizon (CH)** as:

> **the temporal and causal distance over which the consequences of a contemplated computational action can be projected with sufficient reliability for a specified purpose, under stated uncertainty and constitutional conditions.**

Conceptually:

\[
CH(a,p)=\max t
\]

such that consequence projections for action \(a\) remain sufficiently reliable for purpose \(p\).

This construct has obvious neighbors in forecasting, planning and control. Its proposed distinctiveness lies in coupling epistemic reach to an authorization boundary.

The questions are different:

> How far can the system see?

and:

> How far should the system be permitted to act?

---

# 23. RoR Is Not Consequence Horizon

Risk-of-Repair and Consequence Horizon are related but distinct.

**CH** asks: How far can consequence be projected with sufficient reliability?

**Calibration** asks: How wrong are those projections likely to be?

**RoR** asks: What is the consequence if the action or projection is wrong?

**Reversibility** asks: Can the action be undone, and at what cost?

**Authority** asks: Who may legitimately permit the action?

These contribute to a constitutional action decision.

A candidate relation is:

\[
CH \times Calibration \times Reversibility \times RoR \times Authority \rightarrow Constitutional\ Action\ Boundary
\]

This is a research surface. It is not a validated production formula.

The current evidence record does not establish that any existing experiment or production instrument measures CH itself.

Adjacent consequence-projection and risk machinery must not be described as CH measurement without a genuine horizon-indexed instrument.

# 26. Free Cognition. Bounded Agency.

Constitutional systems ordinarily govern acts.

Thought may precede action. Intent may shape action. Reasoning may explain action.

But the constitutional event is the point at which capability crosses into consequential exercise.

\[
Cognition \rightarrow Decision \rightarrow Authority\ Boundary \rightarrow Action \rightarrow Consequence
\]

This yields a complementary framing to model-level alignment:

> **What must remain true when cognition becomes consequential action?**

Invariant Intelligence does not require every internal hypothesis, simulation or possible line of reasoning to be constitutionally admissible as an action.

Capability may require the opposite.

An intelligence may need to model adversarial strategies without adopting them, consider dangerous actions without performing them, explore contradictory hypotheses, simulate undesirable outcomes and reason through possibilities that would be constitutionally prohibited if enacted.

The constitutional boundary therefore need not sit at cognition itself. It may sit at the conversion of cognition into agency.

Hence:

> **Free Cognition. Bounded Agency.**

This is not a claim that artificial agents possess human legal personhood or human rights.

It is an architectural proposition:

> **Reasoning requires sufficient exploratory freedom for capability to expand, while consequential action requires constitutional authority.**

Or:

\[
Capability\ may\ explore
\]

while:

\[
Authority\ determines\ what\ may\ act
\]
