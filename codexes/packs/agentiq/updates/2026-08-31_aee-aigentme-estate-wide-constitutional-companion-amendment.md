## 0. Ruling

The estate-wide constitutional companion model is hereby made explicit:

> **aigentMe is the person's primary constitutional companion across the estate, at every level of sophistication and expertise.**

The person does not outgrow aigentMe by becoming expert, professional or highly autonomous.

What changes with expertise is the **experience altitude** of the companion relationship: information density, explanation depth, degree of scaffolding, exception handling, specialist-agent use and delegated operational autonomy may all change. The person-level constitutional companion relationship remains.

The companion role is also **delegable**:

> **A person may delegate another eligible agent to operate as aigentMe, within the person's bounded authority, disclosure and delegation constraints.**

This delegation changes who is performing the companion role. It does not create a second person, a second constitutional continuity anchor, or a second source of authority.

---

# 1. Why this is estate-wide

AEE-001B establishes that the experience belongs to the person and the journey rather than the interface. That proposition requires a persistent person-centred coordinating relationship above individual domains and surfaces.

Bridges organize domains. Cartridges organize capability. Specialist agents organize expertise. Journey Spine organizes progression. AEE determines the appropriate experience.

aigentMe provides the persistent constitutional companion relationship through which these can be orchestrated around the person.

Canonical compression:

> **The Bridges organize domains. Specialist agents organize expertise. aigentMe organizes the estate around the person.**

This applies in:

- metaMe;
- Financial Services Bridge;
- Knights Bridge;
- Constitutional Internet Bridge;
- other present and future Bridges;
- Cartridges;
- Companion surfaces;
- mobile/native experiences;
- embedded or externally hosted experiences;
- MCP/harness-mediated interactions;
- mixed human-agent workflows.

The visible manifestation of aigentMe may differ by surface. The constitutional companion relationship remains person-centred and continuous.

---

# 2. Separation of responsibilities

The following responsibilities must remain distinct.

### Journey Spine

Determines:

> **Where are we going, where are we now, and what genuinely has to happen?**

### Experience Models / Matrices / ExQube / Experience Guide

Describe experiential possibilities, evidence and recommendations.

### AEE

Determines:

> **What is the Next Best Experience for this participant in this context?**

AEE owns bounded experience decisioning and projection. It does not become the person's companion relationship.

### aigentMe

Orchestrates the person's experience of that NBE across domains, surfaces, agents and journeys.

It answers operationally:

- which domain or Bridge is relevant now;
- what experience altitude should be used;
- what material should be surfaced;
- which specialist agent should participate;
- which modality or composition is appropriate;
- whether to remain in the current environment or hand off to another;
- how to maintain continuity before and after the handoff;
- what exceptions or consequences deserve the person's attention.

### AgentiQ

Executes bounded intelligence and action through agents, tools, services and workflows.

### Specialist agents

Provide domain expertise or bounded execution, for example MoneyPenny in Financial Services.

### Constitutional Computing / CTP target architecture

Governs consequential authority and canonical state transition.

Canonical relationship:

```text
Person
  ↓
aigentMe constitutional companion relationship
  ↓
Journey + context + ExQube
  ↓
AEE → Next Best Experience / ExperienceProjection
  ↓
aigentMe orchestrates realization
  ↓
Bridge / Cartridge / surface / specialist agent
  ↓
AgentiQ execution where required
  ↓
Constitutional authorization where consequential
  ↓
Receipt / consequence / evidence
  ↺
```

Canonical compression:

> **AEE determines the Next Best Experience. aigentMe orchestrates the person's constitutional experience of it.**

# 3. Ubiquity does not mean visual persistence

The constitutional companion relationship is ubiquitous. A floating chat window or avatar does not have to be.

For a novice, aigentMe may be highly visible and explanatory:

- orientation;
- prompts;
- contextual help;
- explicit NBE guidance;
- step-by-step transitions.

For an expert, aigentMe may become quieter and more executive:

- exception management;
- high-signal alerts;
- consequence awareness;
- specialist-agent coordination;
- delegated-work supervision;
- cross-domain opportunity detection;
- concise NBE recommendations.

Therefore:

> **Expertise changes companion altitude, not the existence of the companion.**

The system must not equate sophistication with the removal of person-centred constitutional support.

---

# 4. aigentMe is not merely a specialist agent

Specialist agents organize intelligence around a domain or function.

aigentMe organizes the experience around the person.

This is a different architectural responsibility.

A specialist may know more than the default aigentMe implementation about a particular domain. That does not give the specialist independent person-level authority or global access to the person's contexts.

The companion role must remain bounded by:

- personhood;
- active persona;
- relationship/domain;
- Journey state;
- disclosure policy;
- mandate/delegation;
- authority;
- Standing where relevant;
- conversation/workflow-specific context;
- constitutional consequence controls.

Coherent presence must not become indiscriminate context collapse.

---

# 5. The companion role is delegable

The architecture distinguishes the **aigentMe constitutional companion role** from any one software agent implementation.

Conceptually:

```text
PERSON
  ↓
constitutional companion role: aigentMe
  ↓
┌───────────────────────────────┐
│ default aigentMe implementation│
│ OR                             │
│ another duly delegated agent  │
└───────────────────────────────┘
```

A person may choose another eligible agent to perform some or all of the companion role within the authority they delegate.

Examples could include:

- a specialist executive agent acting as the person's primary companion for a venture context;
- a Financial Services specialist operating the companion role within a bounded financial relationship;
- a future trusted personal agent selected by the person.

The role delegation MUST NOT:

- change the human person as constitutional anchor;
- manufacture authority the person does not hold;
- silently widen disclosure across personas or relationships;
- bypass Journey dependencies;
- bypass consequential authorization;
- convert specialist expertise into person-level sovereignty;
- create competing canonical state.

Delegation should remain explicit, bounded, inspectable and revocable according to the estate's delegation architecture.

---

# 6. Delegated agent acting-as-aigentMe

An agent acting as aigentMe should be understood as occupying a bounded constitutional companion role, not impersonating the person.

The operating relationship is:

```text
Person
  ↓ delegates companion mandate
Agent X acting-as-aigentMe
  ↓
same person anchor
same Journey/AEE contracts
bounded persona/context disclosure
bounded delegated authority
receipted consequential acts where applicable
```

The agent may orchestrate NBE realization within the delegated envelope. Any consequential act still resolves through the appropriate canonical authorization mechanism.

The person remains able to change which agent occupies the role according to the estate's delegation controls.

---

# 7. Cross-domain orchestration

Because aigentMe is person-centred rather than Bridge-centred, it may orchestrate transitions between domains when the NBE warrants it.

Example:

```text
Creative journey
  ↓
person creates and owns an asset
  ↓
AEE identifies financial understanding as NBE
  ↓
aigentMe introduces an appropriate Financial Sovereignty experience
  ↓
Knights Bridge learning / Financial Services capability
```

The reverse is also possible. A Financial Services journey may legitimately surface creative, communication or narrative capability when that is the relevant NBE.

Domain transitions do not erase domain boundaries. The companion carries continuity while disclosure, authority and consequence remain scoped to the destination context.

# 8. NBE and experience altitude

The AEE may determine a Next Best Experience using context such as:

```text
Intent
+
Journey state
+
Creative capability state
+
Financial capability state
+
Experience evidence
+
Standing
+
Authority
+
Consequence
+
Environment / device / host
────────────────────────────
Next Best Experience
```

The NBE may include an appropriate **experience altitude**.

aigentMe then orchestrates the practical realization of that NBE by selecting or coordinating:

- domain / Bridge;
- material;
- specialist agent;
- experience primitives;
- rendering/composition mode;
- explanation depth;
- action, simulation or practice;
- constitutional handoff;
- return path.

This does not give aigentMe permission to manufacture eligibility. NBE remains recommendation/orchestration; authorization remains constitutional.

# 9. Relationship to Financial Services

The Financial Services Bridge can remain specialist, dense and professional-grade.

Knights Bridge can expose the same underlying Financial Services Runtime at lower or progressively increasing experience altitudes.

aigentMe can coordinate across both without creating two Financial Services engines.

Therefore:

> **The Financial Services Bridge exposes the domain as a professional operating environment. Knights Bridge progressively prepares the person to operate in that same domain. aigentMe maintains continuity across both.**

MoneyPenny remains the primary Financial Services specialist. aigentMe remains the primary person-centred constitutional companion unless the person delegates the companion role differently.

---

# 10. Relationship to Progressive Sovereignty

Progressive Creative Sovereignty and Progressive Financial Sovereignty are domain progression systems.

aigentMe is not the owner of either progression model. It is the person's constitutional companion across them.

It can therefore help the person:

- recognize the most relevant next capability;
- move between creative and financial learning;
- adjust experience altitude;
- invoke appropriate specialist agents;
- preserve cross-Bridge continuity;
- supervise delegated activity;
- understand consequence;
- return to a prior journey without losing state.

This makes aigentMe a critical continuity layer for the Knights Bridge dual-ascent model.

# 11. Estate-wide invariants

The following rules are ratified as AEE companion invariants:

1. **aigentMe remains the person's primary constitutional companion across the estate.**
2. **The person does not outgrow the companion relationship through expertise.**
3. **Experience altitude may change radically; constitutional companionship remains.**
4. **AEE determines the NBE; aigentMe orchestrates its realization around the person.**
5. **Journey Spine governs progression; aigentMe does not fabricate Journey state.**
6. **Specialist agents organize expertise; aigentMe organizes the estate around the person.**
7. **The aigentMe role is delegable by the person to another eligible agent.**
8. **Delegating the role does not delegate more authority than the person actually grants.**
9. **An agent acting as aigentMe acts for the person within a bounded companion mandate; it does not become the person or an independent principal.**
10. **Cross-domain orchestration must preserve persona, relationship, disclosure and constitutional boundaries.**
11. **Recommendation and NBE are not authorization.**
12. **Ubiquity is architectural, not necessarily visual.**
13. **Provider or renderer changes must not sever the constitutional companion relationship.**
14. **No Bridge, Cartridge or specialist agent should need to recreate a separate person-level companion model.**
15. **Companion delegation should remain explicit, inspectable and revocable under the estate's delegation controls.**

The amended AEE model can be summarized as:

> **Personhood supplies continuity. Journey Spine supplies progression. AEE determines the Next Best Experience. aigentMe orchestrates that experience around the person. Specialist agents supply expertise. AgentiQ performs bounded work. Constitutional Computing governs consequential action. Renderers manifest the experience.**

And the estate-wide companion law is:

> **One person, one continuing constitutional companion relationship, many possible delegated agents, many domains and interfaces.**
