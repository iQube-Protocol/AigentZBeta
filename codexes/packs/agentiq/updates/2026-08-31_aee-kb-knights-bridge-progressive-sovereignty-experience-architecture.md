## 0. Executive proposition

Knights Bridge is an adaptive progression environment through which a person can move from curiosity and participation toward increasingly sophisticated forms of creative and financial agency.

Its central proposition is:

> **The underlying capability can remain professional-grade while the experience through which a person reaches it becomes progressively appropriate to their knowledge, confidence, Standing, authority, intent and consequence.**

Knights Bridge therefore does not require a beginner creative system and a beginner financial system that later have to be replaced by professional products.

Instead, it provides a progressive gateway into the same underlying capabilities used by advanced participants.

The person may begin with explanation, guided participation and simulation; progress into practice, creation, ownership and bounded real activity; and eventually operate directly inside professional-grade environments.

The engine does not need to change.

The experience does.

This is the application of the Adaptive Experience Engine to **Progressive Sovereignty**.

# 7. Experience altitude

Knights Bridge introduces **experience altitude** as a first-class AEE concept.

Experience altitude describes the degree of abstraction, scaffolding, explanation and information density through which a capability is presented.

A reference model is:

### Altitude 0 — Orientation

What is this? Why does it matter? What can I safely explore?

### Altitude 1 — Guided Learning

Concepts, examples, contextual explanation and interactive instruction.

### Altitude 2 — Practice

Simulation, sandbox activity, low-risk experimentation and assisted creation.

### Altitude 3 — Participation

Real but bounded capability: small transactions, creative contributions, community participation and attributable outcomes.

### Altitude 4 — Independent Operation

Direct capability access with reduced scaffolding.

### Altitude 5 — Advanced / Professional

Dense information, specialist services, sophisticated tooling and deeper controls.

### Altitude 6 — Delegation / Supervision

The person responsibly delegates bounded work to agents and supervises synthetic capability.

### Altitude 7 — Contribution / Leadership

The person contributes knowledge, provides capability, teaches, creates primitives or helps govern the domain.

These are experience altitudes, not social ranks.

---

# 8. Capability maturity is persistent; experience altitude is situational

A sophisticated participant may still need a lower-altitude experience in an unfamiliar context.

A professional investor encountering a new custody mechanism may need guided explanation.

A novice creator may already possess advanced financial capability.

A highly experienced person making a high-consequence decision may benefit from more explanation than usual.

Therefore:

> **Progression records capability history; context determines current experience altitude.**

The AEE must not equate expertise with a permanent demand for terse or minimally assisted interfaces.

# 9. Adaptive capability release

Progressive Financial Sovereignty makes capability release a major AEE application.

The system may progressively introduce deeper capability based on legitimate evidence such as:

- demonstrated understanding;
- successful practice;
- prior participation;
- Standing;
- qualification;
- authority;
- wallet readiness;
- Journey state;
- risk class;
- declared intent;
- prior outcomes;
- constitutional eligibility requirements.

AEE may determine:

> **This participant appears ready to be introduced to this capability.**

It does not determine:

> **This participant is constitutionally authorized to exercise it.**

Recommendation and authorization remain distinct.

# 12. The aigentMe role is delegable

The constitutional companion role must remain distinct from one permanent software implementation.

The person may delegate another eligible agent to operate as aigentMe within a bounded mandate.

```text
PERSON
  ↓
constitutional companion role: aigentMe
  ↓
┌───────────────────────────────┐
│ default aigentMe implementation│
│ OR                             │
│ delegated eligible agent      │
└───────────────────────────────┘
```

A delegated agent acting as aigentMe:

- acts for the person within the granted companion mandate;
- does not become the person;
- does not become an independent principal;
- does not gain global access merely by occupying the role;
- remains bounded by persona, relationship, disclosure and authority;
- must still use canonical consequential authorization mechanisms.

This allows advanced users to choose a preferred personal agent architecture without fragmenting the person-level continuity model.

# 19. Learning evidence and Standing remain distinct

Learning evidence may capture:

- concepts explored;
- simulations completed;
- demonstrated understanding;
- practice outcomes;
- corrected misconceptions;
- observed confidence;
- repeated successful patterns.

Standing captures constitutional participation/contribution evidence according to its own rules.

They may inform each other where explicitly governed.

They are not synonyms.

Completing a lesson does not create financial authority.

Holding Standing does not imply expertise in every financial discipline.

# 28. Governing rules

1. **One professional-grade capability substrate.** Knights Bridge consumes canonical capabilities rather than forking them.
2. **Progressive Financial Sovereignty is the umbrella financial capability.**
3. **Financial Services Learning is the broad learning capability beneath PFS.**
4. **Crypto, crypto trading and other specialist disciplines sit beneath Financial Services Learning / PFS rather than defining the umbrella.**
5. **Progressively expose complexity; do not weaken capability for beginners.**
6. **Capability maturity and experience altitude are separate.**
7. **Learning is not authority.**
8. **Simulation is not execution.**
9. **Recommendation / NBE is not permission.**
10. **Standing and learning evidence remain distinct.**
11. **Creative and Financial Sovereignty remain distinct but interoperable.**
12. **aigentMe remains the primary constitutional companion at all sophistication levels.**
13. **AEE determines NBE; aigentMe orchestrates the person's experience of it.**
14. **The aigentMe role is delegable to another eligible agent within bounded authority.**
15. **Delegating the companion role does not create a new principal or a second person-level continuity anchor.**
16. **Cross-Bridge orchestration preserves persona, disclosure, Journey and consequence boundaries.**
17. **Professional handoff should preserve personhood, Standing, authority, wallet relationships and canonical runtime state where applicable.**
18. **Progression should produce evidence rather than arbitrary gamification.**
19. **Consequence awareness should increase with consequence.**
20. **The person should never need to abandon the underlying system merely because they became more capable.**

# 29. Architectural compression

Paper III can be reduced to three governing propositions.

> **Do not build a beginner system and an expert system. Build one sovereign capability system that grows with the person.**

> **The Financial Services Bridge exposes the domain as a professional operating environment. Knights Bridge progressively prepares the person to operate in that same domain.**

> **The Bridges organize domains. Specialist agents organize expertise. aigentMe organizes the estate around the person.**

And the dual-sovereignty proposition is:

> **Progressive Creative Sovereignty develops the capacity to create, own and participate in value. Progressive Financial Sovereignty develops the capacity to understand, manage and exercise economic agency over value. Knights Bridge provides the adaptive journey through both.**
