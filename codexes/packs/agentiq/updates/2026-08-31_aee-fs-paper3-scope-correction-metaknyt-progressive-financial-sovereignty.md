### Financial progression hierarchy

The planned third paper must NOT frame crypto, crypto trading, or any other specialist financial discipline as the umbrella progression model.

The canonical hierarchy is:

```text
Progressive Sovereignty
├── Progressive Creative Sovereignty
│   └── creative learning, making, ownership, participation and ascension capabilities
│
└── Progressive Financial Sovereignty
    └── Financial Services Learning Capability
        ├── financial literacy / foundations
        ├── money, payments and settlement
        ├── saving / allocation / financial planning
        ├── investing / markets
        ├── digital assets / crypto
        ├── crypto trading and market participation
        ├── risk / consequence / security
        └── other specialist financial-services disciplines as constituted
```

**Progressive Financial Sovereignty (PFS)** is therefore the umbrella capability and progression machinery that sits alongside **Progressive Creative Sovereignty (PCS)**.

**Financial Services Learning** is a general-purpose learning capability within PFS.

Crypto, digital assets, crypto trading and related disciplines are specialist branches within that broader Financial Services learning capability, not substitutes for it.

## 2. Progressive Financial Sovereignty

Progressive Financial Sovereignty should be treated as a first-class sibling to Progressive Creative Sovereignty.

It is not simply a course, content taxonomy or gamified ladder. It is the machinery through which a person can progressively develop **understanding, competence, agency, participation, responsibility and trusted capability in financial systems**.

The exact ladder stages remain to be designed in the third paper, but the architectural intent is already clear:

> **A person should be able to move from financial unfamiliarity to informed participation, practical capability, bounded execution, deeper specialization and ultimately trusted financial agency without changing constitutional identity or losing continuity of evidence, Standing and experience.**

AEE enables the path to adapt to the person rather than force every participant through a fixed curriculum.

## 3. Financial Services Learning as a general capability

The Financial Services Learning capability should span the domain broadly before specialization.

It may include:

- foundational financial literacy;
- understanding value, money and exchange;
- payments and settlement;
- wallets, custody and security;
- saving, budgeting and allocation;
- markets and investment concepts;
- financial products and services;
- risk, consequence and fraud awareness;
- constitutional financial rights, authority and delegation;
- digital assets and crypto;
- trading;
- DeFi and protocol-specific disciplines where appropriate;
- agentic finance and supervised financial automation;
- advanced / professional specializations as later constituted.

This list is a domain map, not a final curriculum.

The AEE should be able to adapt explanation depth, sequence, modality, practice surfaces, simulation, agent assistance, assessment and next-best learning experiences according to the participant's Journey, experience evidence, demonstrated capability and Standing.
