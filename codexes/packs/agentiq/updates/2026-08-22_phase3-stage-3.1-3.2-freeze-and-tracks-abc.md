## Governing Phase 3 definition

> **Financial Service Orchestration** binds an eligible agent, service, authority, mandate,
> consequence projection, authorisation, execution and observed consequence into one receipted
> service lifecycle.

This is `requestFinancialService()`'s contract, stated as doctrine: every field it threads
(`FinancialServiceRequest`, `ConstitutionalAuthority`, `ConsequenceProjection` when reachable,
`ActionAuthorisation`, `CommerceExecution`, `ObservedConsequence`) is bound into the single
`FinancialServiceOutcome` + `CausalChainRefs` the function returns, and the receipt trail
(`emitActionAuthorisationReceipt`/`emitExecutionReceipt`/`emitConsequenceReceipt`) is the
lifecycle's durable, DVN-anchorable record. A "service" that skips any bound element (e.g. calls
execution without a resolved authorisation, or claims a receipt without a bound execution) is not
Financial Service Orchestration under this definition — it is a defect.
