## The June transition: build the gap

Experience Vibing began with stable, production-grade capabilities in Studio. DevOn extended that principle into development itself:

1. State the intent.
2. Search the Registry for existing capabilities.
3. Reuse a proven capability where it already fulfils the intent.
4. Extend it where the fit is partial.
5. Create only the unresolved delta—the **Intent-to-Registry Gap**.
6. Evaluate the end-to-end consequence, including time to value and risk of repair.
7. Register the proven result so the next builder does not solve the same problem again.

This is circular development. It treats human attention and inference as scarce: do not rebuild what is already proven; build the gap and preserve the evidence.

## The July transition: make coherence executable

Invariant Intelligence made the June architecture constitutional. An invariant is not a slogan; it is a falsifiable condition the system must preserve. DevOn increasingly became the development command centre for finding the gap, identifying the relevant invariants, composing registered capabilities, implementing the delta and preserving evidence of the resulting consequence.

The Polity Passport then separated **personhood** from **identity**. Identities can be contextual and change; the person provides constitutional continuity. Agents act through bounded delegation. Contributions can therefore be attributed to the person without making a university, workspace or agent the owner of that contribution.

## Your capstone now

Your task remains to deliver a working agent or agentic service. The target has become more precise: the result should be admissible as either:

- a capability callable by **DevOn** within the Constitutional Computing development environment; or

It must be Registry-addressable, bounded in authority, tested, evidenced and demonstrable. You are not expected to rebuild the platform. Start with an IRG analysis and build the smallest consequential delta.

## Constitutional credit

Your university grade and your constitutional contribution are deliberately separate.

- Faculty may grade the capstone; faculty cannot directly grant Standing.
- Submission volume, commit count, hours, pages and resubmissions do not earn Standing.
- A verified, evidenced contribution can pass the shared Standing admission gate.
- Attribution uses a public persona commitment rather than exposing the raw persona identifier.
- The contribution and its attribution can persist beyond the course and workspace, so you can point to your place in the platform's lineage.

One implementation boundary remains open: admitted research-contribution signals do **not yet automatically accrue into Standing lanes**. The code explicitly records this as “Slice C.” Until that wiring is completed, IRL receipts and admission decisions preserve honest attribution and eligibility, but must not be represented as an already-issued Standing score.
