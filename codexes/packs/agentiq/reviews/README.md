# Independent External Reviews

**This directory is deliberately separate from `codexes/packs/agentiq/updates/` (the Evidence Agent's own evidence-resolution record) and from `codexes/packs/agentiq/resources/` (primary-source corpora).**

Files here are independent reviews authored by a different hand than whoever produced the evidence record or the manuscript under review — most often an adversarial or merit-to-falsify review of a Threshold research edition. Their evidentiary value depends partly on their not being folded into the same corpus they review: a review committed alongside, and formatted identically to, the evidence record it comments on would blur exactly the distinction (independent external check vs. self-produced evidence) that gives it weight.

**Do not move these files into `updates/` or edit their content to match house style.** Commit them as supplied by the reviewing agent/party, verbatim, with a stable sha256 recorded alongside.
