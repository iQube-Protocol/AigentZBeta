# CIP-003B — Canonical MetaVitruvian · Female Rendering

This is the female canonical rendering of **CIP-003 — Canonical MetaVitruvian**.

It has equal canonical standing with CIP-003A. Its purpose is not to create a second doctrine, but to make explicit that the canonical constitutional subject is not bound to one gendered representation.

The governing rule is:

> **Gender contextualizes embodiment. It does not create personhood.**

And:

> **The form may rotate. The embodiment may change. The person remains.**
