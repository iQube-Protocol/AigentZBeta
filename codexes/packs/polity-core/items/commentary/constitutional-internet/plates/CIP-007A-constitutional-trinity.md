# CIP-007A — The Constitutional Trinity

## Canonical question

> How do we think, organize, and act in service of the constitutional good?

## Principle

A constitutional society operates through three inseparable primitives:

- **Reasoning** to discern truth.
- **Order** to align and govern.
- **Action** to create consequences.

## Central concept

The **Constitutional Trinity** is a closed loop of intelligence and consequence, oriented by invariants and realized through artifacts.

The rendering places **Invariant Intelligence**, **Consequence Engineering** and **Artifacts** inside the Constitutional Bearing Instrument, with a standing ring that moves through experimental, validated, registered, canonical and foundational states.

## Machine summary

A full explanatory constitutional plate that presents Reasoning, Order and Action as an inseparable triad. It uses a navigation-instrument metaphor to show how invariants orient constitutional intelligence, how artifacts express it, and how consequence gives action constitutional meaning.
