# CIP-003 — Canonical MetaVitruvian

The Canonical MetaVitruvian is the Constitutional Internet's primary visual representation of the **person as constitutional subject in the agentic age**.

It places the embodied person at the centre of a constitutional field and composes personhood, individuality, uniqueness, standing, bounded delegation, anonymity, mobility, identity composition, and human–agent partnership around that subject.

## Paired canonical renderings

- **CIP-003A — Male Rendering**
- **CIP-003B — Female Rendering**

The two are equal renderings of one constitutional concept. Neither is the default subject and neither alters the doctrine. The difference is representational, not constitutional.

## Bridge role

Primary: **Constitutional Internet Bridge / View**.

The plate can anchor video and prose that answer: **Who is the Constitutional Internet for?** The answer is the person — before the account, identity, platform, institution, or agent.

## Machine reading

Agents should interpret the MetaVitruvian as a constitutional representation invariant, not as a biometric template, gender classification, identity proof, or claim that any single visual body defines personhood.
