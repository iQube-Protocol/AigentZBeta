# CIP-004 — Government-Grade, Not Government-Dependent

This plate explains the Polity Passport as constitutional infrastructure capable of meeting high assurance and accountability requirements without making the person dependent on a single government, platform, or identity provider for constitutional presence.

Its bridge function is especially strong at the boundary between **View** and **Orient**: it translates the Constitutional Internet from a civilizational argument into a practical question about how a person can participate with privacy, continuity, accountability and portability.

## Interpretive discipline

The plate should be read as constitutional and policy framing. It must not be used to imply that every government, regulator, court, or jurisdiction has formally recognized the Polity Passport. Claims of legal or institutional recognition must remain separately evidenced.
