# CIP-006 — The Constitutional Internet

This is the overview plate for the Constitutional Internet as a living constitutional system rather than a single product, network or credential.

It compresses the relationship among information, agency, verified action, standing, authority, risk reduction and consequential benefit into one visual field.

## Bridge role

This is the strongest candidate to open the **View** stage because it answers the first systems-level question:

> **What is the Constitutional Internet?**

Subsequent video, MetaVitruvian, Passport and Constitutional Agency material can then move from system → subject → constitutional presence → action.

## Interpretive discipline

The plate expresses constitutional architecture and direction. Individual operational claims remain governed by the evidence state of the underlying component. The plate must not be cited as proof that every depicted capability is fully deployed.
