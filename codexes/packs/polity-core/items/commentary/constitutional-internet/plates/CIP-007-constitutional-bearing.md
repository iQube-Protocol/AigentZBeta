# CIP-007 — Constitutional Bearing

**Constitutional Bearing** is the orientation instrument of the Constitutional Internet plate set: a visual model for locating reasoning, order and action inside a constitutional field and for showing how invariant intelligence becomes consequential through artifacts.

The family has two equally canonical renderings:

- **CIP-007A — The Constitutional Trinity: Reasoning · Order · Action** — the full explanatory plate, including canonical question, principle, central concept, related plates and design principles.
- **CIP-007B — Constitutional Bearing Instrument: Navigate the Atlas** — the focused instrument rendering, exposing the layered navigation model directly.

## Canonical propositions

- **Reasoning** discerns truth and understanding.
- **Order** aligns relationships and enables coordination.
- **Action** creates consequence.
- The three are inseparable in constitutional operation.
- Invariants orient the field; artifacts make reasoning and order consequential.
- Constitutional knowledge can move through states of standing: experimental, validated, registered, canonical and foundational.

## Machine interpretation

The Bearing Instrument is not a literal navigation sensor or measurement device. It is a constitutional orientation model. Its headings, bearings, altitude, ground speed and track values are representational interface language used to make constitutional position and movement legible. They must not be interpreted as empirical measurements unless a future implementation explicitly binds them to measured state.

## Bridge use

CIP-007 is suitable for **View** when introducing the Constitutional Internet as a navigable field rather than a static doctrine, and for **Orient** when helping a person locate their current relationship to reasoning, order and action before a constitutional act.

Exact placement remains a composition decision; canonization does not lock the plate to one bridge stage.
