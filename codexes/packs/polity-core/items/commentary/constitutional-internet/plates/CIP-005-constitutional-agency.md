# CIP-005 — Constitutional Agency

This plate explains how humans, agents and institutions participate in consequential action without collapsing capability, representation and authority into one thing.

It is the primary visual companion to the Constitutional Internet's doctrine of **bounded delegation** and the distinction between a constitutional principal, a representative exercising mandate, and helpers that extend capability without acquiring independent authority.

## Bridge role

- **View:** explains how constitutional action works.
- **Act:** supports the transition from contextual agent connection to possible future mandate.
- **Stand:** makes the relationship between verified action, consequence and standing legible.

The plate does not itself confer authority. In particular, connecting an agent through MCP, exposing context to an agent, or allowing an agent to assist with reasoning does not constitute delegation.
