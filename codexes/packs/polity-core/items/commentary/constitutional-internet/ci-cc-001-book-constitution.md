**Status:** Proposed for canonisation in Polity Core / Constitutional Commentary

**Author:** Dele Atanda
**Work:** *The Constitutional Internet: The Last Human Frontier*

> To make the person visible to themselves, the threshold visible before them, and the polity imaginable as a society they already possess the right and agency to enter.

## Canonical Rules

### Rule 0 — The Person Is the Constitutional Unit

> The person is the irreducible constitutional subject, entity, and unit of the Constitutional Internet.

### Rule I — Sovereign Personhood Precedes Sovereign Identity

> The book must never describe sovereign identity without grounding it in sovereign personhood.

### Rule II — Representation Must Never Supersede Embodiment

> The person must never disappear behind their representation.

### Rule III — Constitutional Invariants Are Non-Negotiable

> Structural invariants may be investigated. Constitutional invariants must be observed.

### Rule IV — Rights Remain Invariant; Their Exercise Remains Accountable

> A right protects legitimate human agency; it does not confer immunity from consequence.

### Rule V — The Constitutional Internet Is Present Tense

> The Constitutional Internet is here. The threshold has been crossed.

### Rule VI — Quantum Change Raises the Constitutional Stakes

> The quantum transition demonstrates that the next Internet requires a change in computational and constitutional paradigm.

### Rule VII — The Polity Is the Embodiment of the Constitutional Internet

> The person is the constitutional unit; the polity is the constitutional society formed through that order.

### Rule VIII — The Polity Papers Are the Doctrinal Substrate

> The Polity Papers codify the doctrine; the book makes the public constitutional argument.

### Rule IX — Constitutional Plates Are Knowledge-Compression Instruments

> Plates are structural visual invariants, not decoration.

### Rule X — metaKnyt Is a Bridge, Source, and Test Bed

> The source ontology must include metaKnyt as a bridge between Volume I and Volume II and as a continuing constitutional media environment.

### Rule XI — The Book Must Remain Human

> Write with the authority of a constitution and the accessibility of a story told from one human being to another.

### Rule XII — The Book Must Carry the Reader Across the Threshold

> The purpose of the book is to carry as many readers as possible across the threshold into the polity.

## Governing Thesis

- The person is the constitutional subject of the Constitutional Internet.
- Personhood is its protected condition. Human agency is personhood in action.
- The Constitutional Internet makes human rights operational as personhood rights through constitutional invariants, proof, bounded agency, and standing.
- The Constitutional Internet is here. Its strength is participatory.
- The Constitutional Internet is the order. The polity is the society it brings into being.
- The purpose of the book is to carry as many readers as possible across the threshold into the polity.
